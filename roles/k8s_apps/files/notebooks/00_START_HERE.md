# DIGITAfrica Federated Learning Workshop

## Your group workspace

Use the JupyterHub username assigned to your group:

| Login | Federated-learning group |
|---|---|
| `group_01` | Group 01 |
| `group_02` | Group 02 |

Your group has its own persistent workspace. Files you create or edit here are
not visible in the other group workspace.

## Workshop boundary

- Work only inside this JupyterLab workspace.
- Do not use terminals, Kubernetes commands, or server addresses.
- The organiser controls the shared Flower aggregation server.
- The practical activity will run one fixed local client per group.

## Start here

Open `01_Group_Workspace.ipynb`, run its first cell, and confirm that the
displayed JupyterHub username is your assigned group login.
